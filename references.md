## 1. Dataset Reference
* **Citation**: Efron, B., Hastie, T., Johnstone, I., & Tibshirani, R. (2004). **Least Angle Regression**. *The Annals of Statistics*, 32(2), 407-499.
* **DOI**: [10.1214/009053604000000067](https://doi.org/10.1214/009053604000000067)
* **Description**: This is the original publication that introduced the diabetes cohort dataset. It is widely used to evaluate linear modeling, feature selection, and regularization algorithms.

## 2. Regularization Methodologies
### Lasso Regression (L1 Penalty)
* **Citation**: Tibshirani, R. (1996). **Regression Shrinkage and Selection via the Lasso**. *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B (Methodological)*, 58(1), 267-288.
* **Link**: [JSTOR Article Link](https://www.jstor.org/stable/2346178)
* **Description**: This paper introduces the Lasso (Least Absolute Shrinkage and Selection Operator), outlining how L1 regularization yields sparse models by forcing coefficients to zero.

### Ridge Regression (L2 Penalty)
* **Citation**: Hoerl, A. E., & Kennard, R. W. (1970). **Ridge Regression: Biased Estimation for Nonorthogonal Problems**. *Technometrics*, 12(1), 55-67.
* **DOI**: [10.1080/00401706.1970.10488634](https://doi.org/10.1080/00401706.1970.10488634)
* **Description**: The foundational paper introducing Ridge Regression (L2 regularization) to stabilize Ordinary Least Squares solutions under severe multicollinearity.

## 3. Multicollinearity & Variance Inflation Factor (VIF)
* **Citation**: O'Brien, R. M. (2007). **A Caution Regarding Rules of Thumb for Variance Inflation Factors**. *Quality & Quantity*, 41(5), 673-690.
* **DOI**: [10.1007/s11135-006-9018-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11135-006-9018-6)
* **Description**: Discusses VIF mechanics, calculations, and the diagnostic boundaries (VIF thresholds of 5 and 10) in regression models.
